👩‍💻 WHO AM I? 👩‍💻

hi! i’m srishti singh 🌟, a computer science student at the university of washington 🎓 who loves building things that actually matter.

this summer, i interned at uber where i worked on the earner loyalty team, helping build backend systems for driver rewards 🚗. last summer, i was at quadrant technologies, where i co-developed vamsa — an app designed to help families stay connected through stories and culture 🪔.

i have experience designing and developing meaningful applications 🌍, mentoring young coders 👩‍🏫, and encouraging others to bring their ideas to life 💡. when it comes to code, i love working with front-end tools like html, css, javascript, and react — and i’m just as excited about backend languages like java, c#, and python 💻.

check out my work on github, and feel free to email me at ssingh48@uw.edu. also, connect with me on linkedin!
